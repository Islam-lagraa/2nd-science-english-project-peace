palestine and isreal

Gaza’s people deserve a future

What is happening in Gaza

For generations, Palestinians have been told to wait for freedom. In Gaza, children have grown up behind a blockade, and now under bombs.

01

Where it comes from

Israelis and Palestinians have fought over land and rights for more than 70 years. Palestinians have lived under Israeli occupation for decades. Since 2007, Gaza has been under a blockade, so people cannot move, work or trade freely. Many Palestinians say they have no freedom and no hope. About 2.1 million people live in Gaza.

02

Israel's attacks on Gaza

Israel has attacked Gaza many times, and since October 2023 it has fought a huge war there, with bombing and a ground attack. Whole neighborhoods have been destroyed. A top UN official said Gaza had become unfit to live in. Families have been forced to leave their homes again and again.

03

The people of Gaza

Gaza’s health ministry says more than 71,000 Palestinians were killed and over 171,000 injured between October 2023 and January 2026. Thousands of the dead are children. Killing innocent people, including children, is wrong.

04

Hunger and lost homes

The UN says most people in Gaza lack enough food, shelter, clean water and toilets. Experts expected about 101,000 cases of acute malnutrition in young children between October 2025 and October 2026. Children cannot grow or learn safely like this.

05

A hollow ceasefire

A ceasefire began in October 2025, but the peace is weak. More aid is entering Gaza, but it is still not nearly enough. The UN also reported that thousands more Palestinians were killed after the ceasefire began.

06

What peace needs

The attacks on Gaza’s civilians must stop. Aid must reach Gaza freely, and rebuilding must start. Palestinians need an end to the blockade, real freedom, and a safe future for every child. Real peace needs fair talks and respect for human life and international law.
